ML 1.09 — Writing Equivalent Fractions Using Multiplication and Division

Generate equivalent fractions by multiplying or dividing the numerator and denominator by the same number.

Worked Example

3 × 28 × 2 = 616

Because both the numerator and denominator were multiplied by the same factor, 3/8 and 6/16 are equivalent.

10 ÷ 520 ÷ 5 = 24

Division works the same way when the numerator and denominator share the divisor.

Key takeaway: Multiply or divide both the numerator and denominator by the same number to generate an equivalent fraction.
