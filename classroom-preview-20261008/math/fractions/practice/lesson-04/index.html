Fractions as Sums

ML 1.04 • Keep the denominator. Break the numerator into parts.

Two Worked Examples

Review the examples, or start when you are ready.

Example 1: 5/3

Thirds stay thirds even after the point passes 1.

5/3 = 1/3 + 4/3
5/3 = 2/3 + 3/3
Example 2: 6/10

Different numerator splits can reach the same point.

6/10 = 3/10 + 3/10
6/10 = 4/10 + 2/10

The denominator names the size of every part. The numerator tells how many of those parts reach the point.
