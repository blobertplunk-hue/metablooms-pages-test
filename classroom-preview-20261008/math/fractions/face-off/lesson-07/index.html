Lesson 7 • At the Same Point

Hit the target at the same point.

Use aligned number lines to generate and identify equivalent fractions. Fractions are equivalent when they are the same distance from 0, even when the number lines are partitioned differently.

1Generate equivalent fractions on the number line.
2Determine whether fractions are equivalent using number-line location and other reasoning you already know.
3Explain how the number lines show the fractions land at the same location.
