Lesson 11 • Same numerator or denominator

Same pieces? Same number of pieces?

Compare fractions by thinking about what the numerator and denominator mean. Use equal-length fraction strips when they help, then explain why your comparison makes sense.

1If the denominators are the same, the pieces are the same size. Compare how many pieces the numerators name.
2If the numerators are the same, there are the same number of pieces. Compare the size of those pieces.
3Build strips, correct a common misconception, and explain your thinking before choosing <, =, or >.
