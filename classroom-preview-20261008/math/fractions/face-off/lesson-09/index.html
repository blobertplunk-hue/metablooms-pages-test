Lesson 9 • Generalizing about equivalent fractions

See what the equation means.

This lesson connects visual fraction models to multiplication and division. You will notice patterns, partition parts into smaller parts, combine equal groups, and then use the equations independently.

1Notice & Wonder: study equivalent-fraction equations and identify the pattern.
2Partition the Parts: multiply the numerator and denominator by the same number and connect the equation to the model.
3Combine Equal Groups: divide the numerator and denominator by the same number and connect the equation to the model.
