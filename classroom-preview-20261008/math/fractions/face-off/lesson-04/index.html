All Kinds of Fractions

Representing Fractions Greater Than or Less Than 1. Represent the same fraction in different ways by decomposing it into sums with the same denominator.

1Warm up with the lesson’s four True or False? comparisons.
2Use models to represent 4/3 and decide whether 2/3 + 2/3 represents the same fraction.
3Decompose number-line fractions such as 6/4, 5/8, 8/6, 3/2, and 9/5 in more than one way.
4Finish with the exact Lesson 4 independent check at 5/4.
Lesson idea: Fractions can be represented as sums of unit fractions, non-unit fractions, or a combination of both, as long as the addends use the same denominator and total the original fraction.
